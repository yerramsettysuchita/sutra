THE AMBIGUOUS CODE HAZARD
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    section number 324 means:
      IPC 324, Hurt by dangerous weapon
      BNS 324, Mischief causing damage

    This hazard does not manifest in this corpus. IPC 427, whose
    successor number collides with IPC 324, has no cases here
    because the generator's modus operandi families do not cover
    mischief. The collision is real in the law and the guard is
    built, but the measured harm from it in this run is zero and
    is reported as zero.
