    PH is a strict subset of P4. Truncating a token to four characters
    can only merge blocks, never split them, so PH+P4 equals P4 exactly
    and the full token key contributes nothing once the prefix key is
    present. It is kept as a diagnostic and not shipped.

    That leaves one real choice, and it is a cost decision.

      PH+TR    2,629,283 pairs   97.64% complete
      P4+TR    3,194,221 pairs   98.12% complete

    P4+TR costs 564,938 more candidate pairs, 21.5% more
    scoring work, to recover about 51 more true pairs. That is
    roughly 11,077 pairs scored per true pair gained.

    We take it. The ceiling constrains every layer downstream and cannot
    be recovered later, while scoring cost is a batch job that runs
    overnight. That is the same asymmetry argument as ADR 004. Shipped
    scheme is P4 plus TR.

    Caveat on these numbers. The corpus draws names from 58 given and
    28 patronymic forms, so it carries about 100 distinct folded tokens
    across 7,600 rows. Real Karnataka has orders of magnitude more, so
    phonetic blocks here are far larger than they would be in the field
    and this reduction ratio is pessimistic. Pairs completeness is not
    affected by that, which is why it is the figure we carry forward.
