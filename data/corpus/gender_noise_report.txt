THE GENDER CHANNEL, AGAINST A FIELD THAT IS NOT CLEAN
==============================================================================

    ADR 028 measured this channel on a corpus where recorded gender
    could not be wrong, because the generator copied it verbatim onto
    every row. The zero true pairs lost was guaranteed, not found.
    This sweeps the error rate. Rate 0.000 reproduces the original
    claim so the overstatement is visible rather than merely admitted.

==============================================================================
WHAT THIS SAYS
==============================================================================

    At a clean field, the original claim, the channel eliminates
    273,335 false pairs and contradicts 0 true ones.
    That zero is a property of the generator, not of police records.

    At the shipped 1.2% rate it contradicts 255 true pairs across
    60 people, and is still worth +0.0053 F0.5.

    The channel remains positive at every rate swept up to 5%.

    The reason it survives is the asymmetry. A wrong gender on one row
    costs the true pairs involving that row. A right gender on every
    other row rejects false pairs across the whole candidate set, and
    there are three hundred times more of those.
