METHOD A2  transfer m AND the threshold, diagnosing why A fails
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Under A, EM drives the mixing proportion to zero on both tables.
    The threshold is log((1-p)/p), so it goes to 20.7 while the best
    score the model produces is 8.1. Nothing can clear it. The
    transferred weights are not the problem, the re estimated prior is.
